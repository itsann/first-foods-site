First Foods is an iPhone app for parents starting a baby on solid food. It keeps a log of what the baby has tried, suggests the next meals and tracks what is in the freezer. By installing or using it you agree to these terms. They are written to be read, not skimmed: the part that matters most is the next section.

It is a log, not medical advice

First Foods keeps track. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and it knows nothing about your baby's health beyond what you type in. The meals it suggests, the order it proposes allergens in and the ages it shows for foods come from general published guidance and from your own log. They are a starting point for your own decisions and for conversations with your baby's doctor, not instructions.

  • If your baby has eczema, a known allergy, a sibling with a food allergy or any other condition, ask your doctor before starting allergens, whatever the app proposes.
  • When you log a reaction, the app stops suggesting that food and notes it for the doctor. It never tells you to try the food again. Putting a food back on the plan is your decision, to be made only after talking to your doctor, and you are responsible for it.
  • Call emergency services for any serious reaction: swelling of the face or tongue, noisy or difficult breathing, repeated vomiting, or a baby who goes pale or floppy. Do not wait to log it.
  • How a food is cut, cooked and served, and whether your baby is ready for it, is always for you to judge at the table.

Your decisions

Everything the app does follows from what you enter and what you choose. You decide what to offer, when, how it is prepared and whether a food that had a reaction is tried again.
