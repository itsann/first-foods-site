First Foods privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

First Foods is an iPhone app for parents starting a baby on solid food. It keeps a log of what the baby has tried, plans the next meals and tracks what is in the freezer. This page explains what the app stores and where it goes.

The short version: everything you enter stays on your phone and, if you turn on iCloud, in your own iCloud account. We have no server, no accounts and no analytics. We never see your data.

What the app stores

  • Your baby's first name, date of birth and, if you enter it, due date.
  • The feeding log: foods offered, when, how it went, any reaction you record and notes you write.
  • What is in your fridge and freezer, and your settings.

All of this is entered by you. The app does not record amounts eaten, photos, location, contacts or anything about your device.

Where it is kept

The log lives on your phone. It is included in your phone's normal iCloud or computer backup like any other app's data.

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps a copy in the private part of your iCloud account, so the log survives a lost phone and appears on a new one. This uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). The data is yours, stored under your Apple ID, and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. We cannot read it.

Sharing with a partner

You can share the log with one other person, such as the other parent, from Settings. Sharing goes through iCloud's own sharing: the person you invite sees and edits the same log on their phone. Only the people you invite can see it, and you can stop sharing at any time from Settings. Nobody else, including us, is part of that share.

What leaves the phone only when you send it

  • Summary for the doctor. Settings can make a one-page PDF of the log to show a pediatrician. It is created on the phone and goes only where you send it with the share sheet.
  • Importing a logbook. You can import a JSON file from an earlier logbook app. The file is read on the phone and nothing is uploaded.

Notifications

The app can send one evening reminder about what to move from the freezer to the fridge for tomorrow. It is scheduled on the phone itself; no notification server is involved. You can switch it off in Settings or in the iPhone's notification settings.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising software.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No third-party code inside the app.

The app's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected" for this reason.

Children

First Foods is made for parents and carers, not for children to use. The only information about a child it holds is what a parent types in: a first name, a date of birth and the feeding log. That information never reaches us.

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Data › Erase everything and start over removes every try, meal, batch and custom food from the phone and, if iCloud is on, from your iCloud copy and from a partner's phone.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from the phone. To remove the iCloud copy as well, open the iPhone's Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Storage, and delete First Foods there.

Medical note

First Foods keeps track; it does not give medical advice. The plan it suggests comes from general guidance and from what you log, not from your baby's health. If your baby has eczema or a known allergy, ask your doctor before starting allergens. Call emergency services for any serious reaction.

Changes to this policy
